# Unfinished & Cut Content — Original Game Playthrough

This file is specifically for documenting things in the original game that felt incomplete, abrupt, or like they were heading somewhere that never arrived. These are the **expansion targets** for the remake — places where the original ran out of time, budget, or scope.

---

## How to Use This File

As you play, note anything that:
- Ends abruptly without resolution
- References something that never appears
- Feels like a placeholder or stub
- Has dialogue that hints at more story
- Has a location that seems underdeveloped
- Has an NPC who disappears without explanation
- Has a quest with an unsatisfying or missing conclusion
- Has gameplay mechanics that seem incomplete or inconsistently applied
